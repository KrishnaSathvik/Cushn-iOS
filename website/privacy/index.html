Privacy Policy

Cushn doesn't collect your data. Your payment list, statuses and settings are stored on your iPhone. They aren't sent to us or anyone else. Cushn has no account, no server, no advertising and no analytics of its own.

Data on your iPhone

Cushn keeps the names, amounts, due days, sections and marks you enter, along with your settings and recent reminders. This stays on your device, and only you decide what goes in it.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, your iPhone schedules them on the device. They show a payment's name and amount, including on the Lock Screen, depending on your iOS notification settings. You can turn them off in Cushn or in iOS Settings at any time.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, iOS may include Cushn's data in your own backup, under Apple's terms. Cushn has no access to those backups.

Crash reports

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. They don't include your payment list.

Links

The Privacy Policy and Support links in Cushn open this website in Safari. Cushn's website stores no payment data and has no analytics, advertising or cookies. Its hosting provider may process technical request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your iPhone until you remove it. You can delete a payment in the app. Deleting Cushn removes its data from your iPhone, apart from any copy in your own backups. Since we never hold your data, there's nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Children

Cushn isn't directed at children, and doesn't knowingly collect anyone's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page.
