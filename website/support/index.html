Support

Need a hand with Cushn? Email support@cushn.app. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version, and leave out anything you'd rather keep private.

Does Cushn pay my bills?

No. Cushn is a checklist. It doesn't send money, connect to banks or check whether a payment went through. A check mark records what you marked.

How do I add a payment?

Tap the gold + button and enter a name, an amount and a due day. Cushn suggests a section, and you can change it.

What happens at the start of a month?

Your list carries forward by itself, and every payment starts unmarked. Earlier months keep their own marks.

How do I change or skip a payment?

Tap the payment's row for Edit, Skip this month and Remove. Edits apply from the month you're viewing onward, and earlier months stay as they are.

How do I reorder sections?

Tap the handle beside a section's name, then drag. Tapping a section's name opens its other options.

Where's Settings?

Tap the Cushn logo at the top of the screen.

Why aren't reminders arriving?

Check that reminders are on in Cushn's Settings, and that notifications are allowed for Cushn in iOS Settings. Open Cushn now and then, so later reminders stay scheduled.

What's the yearly estimate?

This month's amount multiplied by 12. It's an estimate, and real months can differ when payments change or are skipped.

Does my list sync or back up?

Cushn doesn't sync between devices. Your list lives on your iPhone, so if the iPhone is lost or the app is deleted, Cushn can't recover it.

Company names and logos

Company names and logos belong to their owners and only help you recognize a payment. Cushn isn't affiliated with or endorsed by them.
