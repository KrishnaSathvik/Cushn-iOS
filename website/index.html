Cushn is a simple monthly checklist for your bills, subscriptions, rent and cards. No bank connection. No account.

Coming soon to the App Store

One list, every month

Add a name, an amount and a due day. Your list carries forward by itself, and each month keeps its own marks, so November never changes December.

Know your monthly total

See what's scheduled, what you've marked paid and what's left, where your payments go, and how this month compares with previous ones.

Every due date in one place

Colored dots show how much falls on each day. Tap a date to see what's scheduled.

Reminders, without the noise

Choose a reminder three days before, on the due date, or both. Once you've marked a payment, its reminders stop. They're optional, and everything else works without them.

Your list stays yours

  • On your iPhone

    Your list is stored on your device. Cushn has no account and doesn't send your data anywhere.

  • No bank connection

    Cushn never moves money or sees your accounts. A check mark is your own record.

  • Works offline

    Everything works without an internet connection.
